Every book club knows the feeling: someone picks a book, half the group doesn't finish it, and the meeting turns into a chat about something else. Picking well helps more than you'd think.

## Start with what people already want to read

Most readers already keep a want-to-read list. Books that show up on several members' lists are a much safer bet than a title nobody has heard of. On Shelf to Stacks, the Book club page compares everyone's lists and shows the overlaps first.

## Check the library before you vote

A book with a 40-person hold list won't reach everyone in time. Favor books with copies on the shelf, or with a short ebook or audiobook wait.

## Keep the shortlist short

Three to five options is plenty. More than that and the vote splits.

## Rank, don't just vote

With a ranked vote, everyone orders the shortlist from favorite to least favorite. The winner is the book most people are happy with, not just the one with the loudest fans. Start a vote from your club's page and share the link.
