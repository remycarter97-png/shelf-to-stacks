You don't need a window seat or a spare room. A reading corner is just a spot that tells your brain: this is where we slow down. Here's how to make one with what you already have.

## Start with the seat

Pick the most comfortable chair you own and give it a permanent home. If it's an armchair, great. If it's a floor cushion against the wall, also great. What matters is that you want to sit there.

## Get the light right

- Put a lamp just behind your shoulder, on the side opposite your writing hand, so the page is lit without glare.
- Choose a warm bulb (around 2700K). Cool white light feels like an office.
- A small clip light is enough for a corner with no outlet nearby.

## Keep everything within reach

A side table, a stool or even a sturdy box works. It holds your tea, your bookmark and the book you're reading next. Add a throw blanket and you have the whole kit.

## Give library books their own shelf

Pick one small shelf or basket just for borrowed books. You'll always know what's due back, and fewer library books get lost under the sofa. When the basket empties, it's time to check your list for what's on the shelf at the library.

## Add one living thing

A plant (a pothos is very forgiving), a cat who agrees to share, or a vase of whatever's in season. It makes the corner feel looked after.
